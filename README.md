# DeepFenceHello everyone,

 Have you ever wondered what happens to AI models—especially advanced analytical or medical (AM) models—once they are deployed in production environments?

These models, used by customers, partners, and vendors across the world, are valuable assets. However, deploying them also introduces major challenges:
How do we protect sensitive data?
How do we prevent model theft or tampering?

This is where our solution comes in—**DeepFence**.

Hi, I’m Aishwarya, and our team has designed an agent-based AI framework that provides real-time protection and analytics for your models.

This is especially crucial in high-risk, high-value domains like healthcare, where models—such as heart attack predictors, dental diagnostics, and more—can cost millions to develop. In the wrong hands, they can cause not only financial loss but also serious harm to patient health and public trust.

1. Data Security
We encrypt and compress training and retraining datasets to prevent tampering and preserve data integrity throughout the model lifecycle.

2. Model Security (Anti-Theft)
Models are protected with authentication layers such as passwords or certificates, ensuring only authorized access and preventing misuse or theft.

3. Telemetry Security
We securely log and monitor model usage, performance, and retraining events—enabling compliance, auditability, and real-time anomaly detection.

With DeepFence, organizations can deploy AI with confidence—knowing their data, models, and users are protected every step of the way.
